## Join the AWTP-Lab!

The **Advanced Water Treatment Plant (AWTP) Lab** is always looking for highly motivated and passionate researchers to join our group. 

We welcome applications for:
* **Undergraduate Researchers**
* **M.S. and Ph.D. Students**
* **Postdoctoral Researchers**

If you are interested in developing innovative water treatment technologies, advanced membrane processes, and resource recovery systems, please send an email to Prof. Youngjin Kim with your **CV** and a brief description of your research interests.
