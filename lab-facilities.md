The **Advanced Water Treatment Plant (AWTP) Lab** is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities, customized bench-scale systems, and analytical instruments to conduct comprehensive research in membrane technologies, advanced oxidation, and water quality analysis.

## Membrane & Process Evaluation Systems

* **RO/NF Performance Evaluation System** (RO/NF 성능 평가 장비)
* **MD/MC Performance Evaluation System** (MD/MC 성능 평가 장비)
* **BPED/ED Performance Evaluation System** (BPED/ED 성능 평가 장비)
* **CDI Performance Evaluation System** (CDI 성능 평가 장비)
* **UV-based Advanced Oxidation Performance Evaluation System** (UV 기반 고도산화 성능 평가 장비)

<br>

## Analytical & Characterization Instruments

* **Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM)** (주사전자현미경)
* **Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS)** (가스크로마토그래피 질량분석기)
* **Fourier-Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FT-IR)** (푸리에 변환 적외선 분광기)
* **Total Organic Carbon (TOC) Analyzer** (총유기탄소 분석기)
* **Ion Chromatography (IC)** (이온 크로마토그래피)
* **Atomic Absorption Spectrophotometer (AAS)** (원자흡광광도계)
* **Thermogravimetric Analyzer (TGA)** (열중량분석기)
