The **Advanced Water Treatment Plant (AWTP) Lab** is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities, customized bench-scale systems, and analytical instruments to conduct comprehensive research in membrane technologies, advanced oxidation, and water quality analysis.

## Membrane & Process Evaluation Systems

* **RO/NF Performance Evaluation System** 
* **MD/MC Performance Evaluation System** 
* **BPED/ED Performance Evaluation System** 
* **CDI Performance Evaluation System** 
* **UV-based Advanced Oxidation Performance Evaluation System** 

<br>

## Analytical & Characterization Instruments

* **Scanning Electron Microscopy (SEM)** 
* **Gas Chromatography-Mass Spectrometry (GC-MS)** 
* **Fourier-Transform Infrared Spectroscopy (FT-IR)**
* **Total Organic Carbon (TOC) Analyzer**
* **Ion Chromatography (IC)**
* **Atomic Absorption Spectrophotometer (AAS)** 
* **Thermogravimetric Analyzer (TGA)**
